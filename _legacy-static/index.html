Who We Are

A Centre for the Symbiocene

The Symbiocene is the era we're trying to build — one where human life grows in symbiosis with the rest of the living world, instead of at its expense. Ulivu Foundation was founded by Seena, an educator and researcher, to turn that idea into everyday practice: in apartment courtyards, school grounds, forest ranges and village panchayats across Karnataka.

We work as connectors as much as doers — bringing together residents, students, foresters, panchayats and fellow non-profits around three commitments: Conservation, Research, and Community.

Conservation

Tree plantation drives, forest clean-ups and habitat protection campaigns across the Western Ghats and urban Bengaluru.

Research

Studying waste systems, water security and rural livelihoods to design solutions grounded in evidence, not guesswork.

Community

Working shoulder to shoulder with schools, universities, forest departments and local government to make change stick.

What We Do

Six ways we show up for the planet

From a single sapling to a policy campaign, our programs meet the environment crisis at every scale.

🌳

Tree Plantation & Urban Forestry

Partnering with the Bruhat Bengaluru civic body on large-scale sapling drives — including a Kempegowda Jayanti tree-plantation push that joined BDA's bid for a Guinness World Record of 1.5 million saplings across the city in a single day.

♻️

Waste Management & Circular Economy

Piloting Kasa @ Source with apartment communities in Bengaluru West to fix waste segregation at the doorstep, alongside public education on moving from a linear "take-make-waste" model to a truly circular one.

🐘

Forest & Wildlife Conservation

Running litter clean-up drives with the Karnataka Forest Department's Umblebailu Range and joining Wildlife Week jungle walks near the Elephant Camp at Gajanur, Shivamogga.

✊

Advocacy & Environmental Justice

Leading the Save Sringeri campaign against a proposed 332 km railway line through the fragile Western Ghats — pressing for cancelled tenders and protected forest corridors over short-term gain.

📚

Education & Community Awareness

Bringing eco-friendly lifestyle workshops, World Environment Day programs and plastic-reduction sessions into schools and Grama Panchayats, from Bengaluru classrooms to Bhadravati villages.

🔬

Research & Capacity Building

Contributing to national platforms on natural farming, water security and rural livelihoods, and hosting webinars with sustainability practitioners to sharpen our own thinking.

Active Campaign

Save Sringeri. Save the Western Ghats.

A proposed Shivamogga–Sringeri–Mangaluru railway line would cut through one of the world's biodiversity hotspots — felling forests, fragmenting wildlife corridors, and destabilising slopes prone to landslides.

  • Lakhs of trees at risk across Someshwara & Kudremukh's critical habitats
  • Increased human-wildlife conflict and habitat fragmentation
  • Landslide and flash-flood risk from blasting and hill-cutting
  • Threats to river systems — the Tunga, Bhadra and Netravati

Our ask: cancel the DPR tender, drop the Sringeri railway alignment, and invest in upgrading existing roads instead.
